# Outcomes and measurement

Funders and partners want to know what changed. Measure what learners can do, not only how many
attended. Keep it short, anonymous where possible, and large-print.

## What to measure

| Measure | When | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Attendance and completion | Every session | Sign-in sheet; completion = attended at least the agreed share of sessions |
| Self-confidence per skill | First and last session | 1-5 scale per task ("I can scan a QR code and open a menu on my own") |
| Observed task check | During the independent try in every session for that session's task, and for all tasks in the final session | Helper ticks "did it alone / with a hint / not yet" per task |
| Use at home | 30 days after (optional) | Short phone or paper check-in: "Since the class, have you...?" |
| Satisfaction and requests | Last session | Two open questions: most useful, still want to learn |

## Pre/post confidence form (example items)

Score 1 (not at all) to 5 (completely). Same wording before and after.

1. I can make the text on my phone bigger.
2. I can scan a QR code and open what it links to.
3. I can pay for parking or a ride with an app if I choose to.
4. I can find a ticket or pass on my phone when I need it.
5. I can join a video call from a link.
6. I can send a photo to one person.
7. I can tell when a call, text or pop-up might be a scam, and what to do.
8. I know where to get help with my phone after this class.

Use only the items for modules actually taught. Match before and after forms with a code printed on the
learner's name tag or folder, not a name. Offer to read the questions aloud.

## Reporting to the partner

A one-page summary: learners enrolled and completing; average confidence before and after per
skill; share who did each observed task alone at the end; top requests; what did not work and what
changes next time; anything the partner should follow up. Report small numbers as counts ("9 of
11"), not impressive-sounding percentages, and do not claim long-term effects you did not measure.

## Honest-claim checks

- Self-reported confidence is not proof of skill; report it next to the observed task checks.
- No before/after comparison claims without both forms.
- Do not use learner quotes or photos without written consent for that use.
