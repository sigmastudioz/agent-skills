# Pricing and cost sheet

Price from real costs and the partner's budget. Every rate below is an input the provider fills
in. The worked example uses round illustrative numbers only; they are not market rates and must
not be quoted as such.

## Cost lines per cohort

| Line | Formula | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instructor delivery | sessions x hours per session x instructor rate | Include arrival and setup time |
| Helpers | sessions x hours x helpers x helper rate | Zero if volunteers; budget a thank-you |
| Preparation | prep hours x instructor rate | Step cards, rehearsing on current phones, demo pages |
| Follow-up and reporting | hours x rate | 30-day check-in, one-page report |
| Printing | pages per learner x learners x cost per page | Large print uses more pages |
| Practice materials | fixed | QR code sheets, sample passes, name tags |
| Loaner devices | devices x (cost / expected cohorts) | Optional; signed out and reset between learners, no personal accounts |
| Travel | trips x cost | Mileage or transit |
| Venue | per session | Often free at libraries and senior centers |
| Insurance | annual premium / cohorts per year | General liability; check partner requirements |
| Background checks | per person | If the partner requires them |
| Admin overhead | percentage of direct costs | Scheduling, invoicing, email |

**Price per cohort** = (sum of lines) x (1 + margin).
**Cost per learner** = price per cohort / learners expected to complete.

Present the partner with the price per cohort, what it includes, the learner count it assumes and
the cost per completing learner. Offer a pilot price for the first cohort only if you can sustain it.

## Worked example (illustrative numbers, not market rates)

Assume 6 sessions x 2 hours, 10 learners, 2 helpers, an instructor rate of R, a helper rate of H,
8 prep hours and 3 follow-up hours.

- Instructor: 6 x 2.5 h (with setup) x R = 15R
- Helpers: 6 x 2.5 h x 2 x H = 30H
- Prep and follow-up: 11R
- Materials, printing, travel, insurance share, overhead: list as their own amounts

So the delivery labour alone is 26R + 30H before materials and margin. Replace R and H with the
provider's actual rates and check the result against the partner's stated budget.

## Pricing checks

- Does the price still work if only 70% of learners complete? Report cost per completing learner.
- Is there a free or low-cost path for learners who cannot pay (sponsor-funded seats)?
- Do the quote, contract and invoice show the same scope and learner count?
- Are payment terms, cancellation (weather, low sign-up) and rescheduling written down?
- No hidden fees to learners; no selling to learners in class.
