# Senior Digital Skills Class
By [Lex from SigmaStudioz](https://github.com/sigmastudioz).

Help someone deliver a small, practical class where older adults leave able to do real
everyday tasks on their own phone: scan a menu, pay for parking, show a ticket, call family on
video, spot a scam. The class is judged by what learners can do unaided afterwards, not by
slides shown or hours billed.

## Rules

1. **Learners keep control of their accounts.** Instructors and helpers never ask for, type, read
   aloud, photograph or store a learner's passwords, PINs, verification codes, card or bank
   details, ID or Social Security/Medicare numbers, and never log in, buy, pay or accept terms
   for a learner. Helpers point and the learner taps; a helper touches the phone only when
   asked, for non-sensitive steps, never for credentials, payments, health data or messages. Details: [learner safety](references/learner-safety.md).
2. **Practise on the learner's own device, with dummy data where money or identity is involved.**
   Use demo menus, practice QR codes, free sample tickets and the instructor's own test accounts
   for walkthroughs; a learner makes a real payment only by their own choice, outside the exercise.
3. **Honest claims only.** Funding figures, programme budgets and "cities will pay this much" claims from
   social media are unverified leads. Never put them in a proposal, price sheet or pitch as fact.
   Funding programmes change; check each one's current status on its official page when you
   use it, and say when you could not. See [funding and partners](references/funding-and-partners.md).
4. **Dignity and pace.** No jargon without a plain-words meaning, no "it's easy", no taking the
   phone away to "just do it". One new skill per segment, repeated until it sticks.
5. **Accessibility first.** Set text size, contrast, captions and hearing settings with each
   learner before teaching apps. Large-print handouts, a quiet room, good light, seating near
   power and Wi-Fi.
6. **Measure outcomes.** Pre/post self-confidence plus observed task checks, collected without
   names or sensitive data where possible. Report what learners can do, including what did not work.
7. **Teach, do not sell.** No upselling devices, plans, apps or paid services to learners in class.
   Recommend free built-in features first; name paid options only when asked, without referral fees.

## Workflow

1. **Frame the offer.** Who are the learners (age range, devices, languages, confidence), who pays
   (city, county office on aging, library, housing provider, the learners themselves) and what the
   partner wants to report. Use the [planning checklist](references/planning-checklist.md).
2. **Pick modules** from the [curriculum](references/curriculum.md) to fit the partner's goals and
   learners' requests; 4-6 sessions of 90-120 minutes is a common starting shape, not a rule.
3. **Design each session** with the [teaching guide](references/teaching-older-adults.md): demo,
   guided practice on own phone, independent try, recap card. Prepare printed step cards for iPhone
   and Android separately.
4. **Set safety boundaries** before the first session and brief every helper
   ([learner safety](references/learner-safety.md)).
5. **Cost and price** with the [cost sheet](references/pricing-and-costs.md); every rate is the
   provider's own input or a labelled assumption.
6. **Find and verify funders and partners** ([funding and partners](references/funding-and-partners.md)).
7. **Run, then measure** with the [outcomes kit](references/outcomes.md); send the partner a short,
   honest results summary.

## Hand off to neighbouring skills when installed

- Grant or RFP response: the small-business **grant-rfp-writer** skill. Client proposal or quote:
  the small-business **proposal-builder** skill. Feed them this skill's curriculum, cost sheet and
  outcomes plan; keep the honest-claims rule.
- Promoting sessions (flyers, sign-ups, library/senior-center calendars): the marketing **events**
  skill.
- Deeper scam and phishing awareness material: the cybersecurity **anti-phishing training
  programme** skill (implementing-anti-phishing-training-program), adapted for consumers and
  plain language. This skill keeps the class's own scam-safety module.

Guidance only: no executable helper, account, tracker or required service. Submitting
applications, signing contracts and contacting funders remain the provider's own actions.
