# Spike title — Non-Technical Doc

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Spike | Shared ID |
| Status / updated | Actual lifecycle state / date |
| Owner / decision maker | Named people or unassigned |
| Technical companion | Link |

## Summary and decision needed

Explain the question, why it matters now and the supported recommendation or lack of a decision.
State whether the recommendation is awaiting approval. Keep this understandable on its own.

## Problem, scope and constraints

Describe who is affected, the current problem, what is included/excluded and the agreed timebox.
Translate constraints into business consequences. Identify proposed assumptions.

## Options considered

| Option | Benefit | Cost / effort and basis | Risk / limitation | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A real option | User or business value | Estimate or measured fact, with uncertainty | Material consequence | Supported, rejected or unknown |

Include the current approach where viable. Explain each rejection without technical shorthand.

## What we learned

Summarize the decisive evidence in plain language and link sources or the technical evidence IDs.
State what was not tested, conflicting findings and the confidence these limits permit.

## Recommendation and impact

Explain the proposed choice, why it follows from the findings, conditions before adoption and the
impact on users, delivery, ongoing work and cost. If inconclusive, say what prevents a decision.
