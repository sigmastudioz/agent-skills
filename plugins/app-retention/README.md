# Returncraft

By **[Lex from SigmaStudioz](https://github.com/sigmastudioz)** · MIT · version 1.1.1.

Design fair retention and ranking for apps, games, stores and assistants from the product's own
data: a daily return-likelihood estimate, nudges timed to the user's real habits, a rescue
action, per-item ranking for feeds and suggestions, results-based choice of photos and
thumbnails, upgrade timing after a win, and speed to first win. Privacy-first (no trackers or
fingerprinting), opt-in, explainable and measured; no dark patterns.

## Changes in 1.1.1

Renamed the display title to **Returncraft**. The stable plugin and install identifier remains
`app-retention`; the retention guidance and behavior are unchanged.

## Changes in 1.1.0

Adds promise → quiz → plan → price onboarding with an honest trial timeline, a one-number test loop with session replay, review asks after a win without gating, free distribution levers, and an avoid list of revenue-tuned difficulty, pricing and review patterns from public app teardowns.

## Changes in 1.0.0

First release. Seven patterns with signals, first constructions, guardrails and checks, drawn
from public creator breakdowns of large consumer apps and treated as hypotheses to test.
